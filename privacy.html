Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Ansonia Theater is a community theater program for students in grades 4 to 6. This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used. We keep it simple because we collect very little.

Information we collect

This website does not have accounts, sign-up forms, or comment sections. We do not ask visitors for names, email addresses, or any other personal information through the site.

If you choose to email one of the contacts listed in the footer, we will receive your email address and whatever you write in your message. We use it only to reply to you.

Website analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they view. This service does not use cookies and does not track individual visitors across websites. It reports general information such as page views, the type of device or browser used, and the approximate country of the visit.

Third-party services

  • Hosting: The site is hosted by Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service.
  • Fonts: The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to download them.
  • Videos: The "Watch" buttons on the Past Shows section open videos on YouTube in a new tab. YouTube has its own privacy policy, and we do not control the information it collects.

Students

This site is meant to be read by families and staff. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website. The cast list may show students' names alongside their roles. If you would like a student's name changed or removed, please contact us and we will update it.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies of its own. The third-party services listed above may use their own cookies or similar technology when you interact with them, such as when you open a video on YouTube.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact Nancy Tingley (ntingley@ansonia.org) or Chris Pytlak (cpytlak@ansonia.org).
